🔍 beacon - Simplify your visa-friendly job search

Download Beacon for Windows

Beacon helps international students and visa holders find work. It scans job boards for positions that offer visa sponsorship. You run this tool on your own computer. Your data stays on your machine at all times. You do not need to create accounts or upload your resume to third-party databases.

📋 Features

  • Automatic Filtering: The software checks job descriptions for visa sponsorship keywords. It removes roles that do not match your needs.
  • Privacy First: No data leaves your laptop. Your search history remains private.
  • Smart Automation: The system uses artificial intelligence to read job postings. It only flags companies that fit your criteria.
  • Organized Tracking: Save your results to a local file or sync them with Google Sheets.

🚀 Setting Up Beacon

You can start using Beacon by following these steps. The process takes about five minutes.

Step 1: Download the Application

Go to the official release page to get the installer for Windows. Visit this page to download the latest version of Beacon.

Step 2: Run the Installer

Locate the downloaded file in your Downloads folder. Double-click the file to start the installation. If Windows asks for permission to run the application, click "Run" or "Yes."

Step 3: Initial Configuration

After the installation finishes, open the Beacon application. The program will ask for your preferred job titles and locations. Type these into the text boxes. The software uses these terms to find relevant openings.

Step 4: Connect Your Tools

If you want to save your progress to a spreadsheet, enter your Google Sheets link in the settings menu. Beacon connects directly to your sheet to store the job data it finds.

🛠 System Requirements

Beacon works on any computer running Windows 10 or Windows 11. You need at least 4 GB of memory and a stable internet connection for the tool to download job listings.

🛡 Privacy and Security

Privacy matters. Many job search sites sell your data to recruiters. Beacon avoids this by keeping your information local. Everything you do happens on your laptop. No central server stores your job history or your personal details. The tool only sends information to the job websites you choose to visit.

💡 Common Questions

Does this cost money? No. This tool is free to use.
